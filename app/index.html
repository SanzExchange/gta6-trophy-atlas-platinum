Roadmap — the route to platinum

Six stages, in the order to play them. Click a stage to filter the checklist. PowerPyx rates the platinum 4/10, about 200 hours, one playthrough.

    Before you start

    • Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter. There is no mission replay and no New Game+.
    • Cheats disable trophies and saving — keep them off your trophy save.
    • One trophy can be missed: Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at Jason’s house in Chapter 1, before that chapter ends.
    • Free-roam continues after the story, so most cleanup can wait until the credits.
