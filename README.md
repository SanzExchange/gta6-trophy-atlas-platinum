Atlas Platinum Leonida — a journey-map guide and tracker for all 55 Grand Theft Auto VI PlayStation 5 trophies: a roadmap to the platinum in six stages, a checklist saved in your browser with export and import, filters by kind and grade, a spoiler setting for story trophies, the one missable trophy flagged, and a cited guide in the atlas's own words for every trophy. Works offline from its own files.

## Run it

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8080 --directory app
# open http://localhost:8080
```
